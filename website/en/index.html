PDFSee

Free & Open Source

Local processing, documents stay on your machine. Open several PDFs in tabs, highlight key passages, add notes, and organize pages in one app.

  • Tabbed reading · Search · Outline
  • Color highlights · Sticky notes · Undo
  • Merge · Split · Extract pages
  • Export PDF to PNG, long image, image-only PDF, Word or PowerPoint
  • macOS & Windows
Download for macOS Download for Windows View source on GitHub
01

Tabbed PDF Reading

Open multiple PDFs in one window and switch between tabs without losing your place. Find a passage with text search, jump by thumbnail or outline, and print directly from the reader.

02

Color Highlights & Sticky Notes

Highlight key passages in five colors. PDFSee remembers your last choice. Add notes anywhere on the page, then edit, move, or delete them. Undo annotation changes and save them to your PDF or to a copy.

03

Merge PDF Combine Files

Add multiple PDF files, drag to reorder, and export as one merged file. Organize contracts, reports, or study materials without online services.

04

Split PDF By Page Count

Set the number of pages per file to split long documents into smaller ones. Support specifying page ranges, outputting to a new folder in your chosen directory.

05

Extract Pages Reorder & Rotate

Enter 1-3, 5, 8-6 to select forward and reverse ranges, enter 3,1,2 to reorder pages. Omit page numbers to delete pages, and rotate exported pages as needed.

06

Export from PDF Images, Word & PowerPoint

Export all or selected pages as individual PNGs, one long image, or an image-only PDF. PowerPoint places one page image on each slide. Word extracts editable text. Original layout, images and table structure are omitted, and scanned pages need OCR first. All these exports run locally.
